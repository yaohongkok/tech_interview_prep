# Questions for HR

## About Managers

1. Can you tell me a little bit about the manager for this role? (Get name of manager)

2. How long has this manager been in this role at this company?

3. What is the retention rate or typical tenure on this specific team compared to the rest of the company?

4. How does this manager deliver feedback and handle performance reviews—is it continuous or mostly formal periodic reviews?

5. What do current team members praise most about working under this manager during skip-level or feedback conversations?
